# Privacy Policy — StudyApp

_Last updated: July 3, 2026_

StudyApp ("we", "us", "the app") helps you turn your course materials into an interactive,
Duolingo-style learning experience. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Who this app is for
StudyApp is intended for users **13 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information,
contact us and we will delete it.

## Information we collect
- **Account information** — your email address (and, if you use Google Sign-In, your Google account
  email and basic profile). Managed by our authentication provider, Supabase.
- **Profile information you provide** — your name, birthdate (used only to confirm you meet the 13+ age
  requirement), and whether you are a student or a self-directed learner.
- **Content you create** — classrooms you create, PDFs/handouts you upload, generated topics, quizzes,
  learning paths, and your chat messages with the in-app AI tutor.
- **Usage and diagnostic data** — basic app events and crash/error reports used to keep the app working.

## How we use your information
- To provide the core features: process your uploaded materials, generate study content, answer your
  questions, and track your progress, XP, and streaks.
- To keep your account secure and enforce fair-use limits.
- To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Third-party services (processors)
We share data with these providers only as needed to run the app:
- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and file storage for your account and content.
- **Google Gemini API** — AI processing of your questions and uploaded materials to generate answers,
  transcriptions, quizzes, and topics. Content you submit to the tutor or upload may be sent to Google
  for processing.
- **Railway** — hosting for our backend server.
- **Sentry** — crash and error reporting (diagnostic data).

Each provider processes data under its own terms and security practices.

## Data retention & deletion
Your content remains until you delete it or request account deletion. To delete your account and
associated data, email us at the address below and we will process your request.

## Your choices
- You can edit or delete your classrooms, handouts, and chat history in the app.
- You can request a copy of your data or its deletion by contacting us.

## Security
We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, row-level access controls, scoped file storage)
to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date, and continued
use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance.
